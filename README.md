🚀 AI Productivity Assistant

A smarter way to organize your tasks, manage your time, and stay productive.

AI Productivity Assistant is a full-stack productivity management application designed to help users organize their daily activities, manage tasks, maintain notes, and plan their schedules in one place. The project aims to integrate AI-powered capabilities to make personal productivity smarter through intelligent task management, schedule recommendations, and productivity insights.

🚧 Project Status: Under Active Development

✨ Features

📊 Dashboard

Get an overview of your daily activities.

Track pending and completed tasks.

View upcoming schedules and deadlines.

Monitor productivity statistics.

✅ Task Management

Create, update, and delete tasks.

Set task priorities and deadlines.

Track task completion status.

Organize daily activities.

📝 Notes Management

Create, edit, and delete notes.

Maintain important information in one place.

Search and access saved notes.

Manage ideas and personal reminders.

📅 Schedule Management

Organize daily schedules and activities.

Plan time for individual tasks.

Keep track of upcoming events.

Manage your daily routine efficiently.

🤖 AI-Powered Productivity (Planned)

AI Chat Assistant: Interact with an AI assistant to manage productivity-related activities.

Chat-to-Task Conversion: Convert natural language instructions into actionable tasks.

Smart Scheduling: Generate optimized daily schedules based on tasks, priorities, and available time.

Productivity Insights: Analyze activity patterns and identify productivity trends.

Predictive Analytics: Use historical activity data to estimate future productivity patterns.
